From Kurdish: to want something deeply, and to wait for it.

How it works

02 / 06

Four steps, then quiet.

Nothing to check every day. WIST speaks only when something changes.

  1. WIST Discover The houses you love, in one place. Following

    01 · Follow

    Follow the houses you love.

    Choose them as you set up WIST, or later in Discover. Their pieces gather on your home page, and search finds any piece by its name or its house.

  2. WIST Coveted Paste a link. WIST keeps watch. Add to Coveted

    02 · Covet

    Covet a piece.

    Tap Covet on a piece, or paste its link from the house’s own shop. Pick the sizes you wear, and WIST watches its price from then on.

  3. WIST A coveted piece just dropped € 550 € 385 −30% · A new low

    03 · The drop

    Hear once, when it drops.

    When a coveted piece falls below the price you coveted it at, with a size you chose in stock, WIST tells you. You set how deep a drop must be: any new low, or from 10, 20 or 30% off. The same low is never announced twice.

  4. WIST Your wardrobe Bought, and already yours. I bought this

    04 · Wear it

    Buy it from the house.

    View on … opens the house’s own site, where buying happens. There is no checkout in WIST. Once it is yours, tap I bought this and the piece moves into your wardrobe.

Dress me

03 / 06

Dressed from what you already own.

Your wardrobe lives in WIST too, and the outfit maker works only with what is in it.

  1. 01

    Photograph a garment.

    WIST’s AI reads the photo and fills in a name, the category, its colours, how formal it is and the occasions it suits. You can correct any of it.

  2. 02

    Ask for an occasion.

    Work, evening, weekend, celebration or travel. Dress me composes outfits from your own pieces, inside your palette and the fit you like.

  3. 03

    One for today.

    Each morning WIST chooses one of your outfits for the day, with the weather where you are in mind.

N° 02 · Your style brief

04 / 06

Fourteen questions, then a brief written for you.

Four short chapters when you begin. Your answers shape every outfit and every alert, and you can change them in your profile at any time.

  1. Chapter I

    Your world

    Where your wardrobe takes you, and what work asks of you.

  2. Chapter II

    Your eye

    This or that, the styles you would wear, and the palettes that feel like you.

  3. Chapter III

    Your fit

    How you like clothes to sit, never your body. Your sizes, and what is missing.

  4. Chapter IV

    Your houses

    The houses you love, three pieces you are waiting for, and when a drop is worth telling you.

For houses

05 / 06

How WIST reads your shop.

WIST reads public product pages the way a careful visitor would, and it would rather ask than assume. These are the rules it follows today.

If you would like to talk about how your house appears in WIST, or would rather it did not appear at all, we would like to hear from you.

Write to WIST

  1. 01

    It says who it is.

    Every request carries the user agent WistCatalogue/1.0 (+https://github.com/zhinoo-zobairi/wist), never a browser’s.

  2. 02

    It honours robots.txt.

    WIST reads your robots.txt first and does not fetch a page it disallows. Rules written for WistCatalogue by name, or for all agents, are followed.

  3. 03

    It takes its time.

    At least ten seconds pass between two requests to the same shop. WIST visits a piece’s page when a reader adds it, then revisits it a few times a day while it is coveted.

  4. 04

    It never buys.

    WIST never adds to a basket, never checks out and never signs in. It reads what the product page already shows: the name, the price and which sizes are available.

  5. 05

    It sends people to you.

    Every piece links back to its page on your own site, and buying always happens there.

  6. 06

    It stops when you ask.

    Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and WIST will stop reading your shop.
