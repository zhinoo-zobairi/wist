Visiting this website

This website runs on WIST's own server in Germany. Visitors reach it through Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, which delivers the pages and protects the server from attacks. To do that, Cloudflare processes your connection data: your IP address, the time of the visit, the page requested, and your browser's technical details.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering this website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Where data reaches the USA, the transfer relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which Cloudflare participates, and on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. How long Cloudflare keeps connection data is described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

WIST's own server keeps no log of visitors.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and loads nothing from other websites: the fonts, photographs and videos are served from WIST's own server.

Writing to WIST

If you write an e-mail to WIST, we use your address and your message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries about working together, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). E-mails are received through our e-mail provider, Google (Gmail). We delete the correspondence once it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). To use these rights, write to the address above. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example [AUFSICHTSBEHÖRDE].
